Somni Referral Programme Terms

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Who runs the programme

The Somni Referral Programme ("Programme") is run by Srinivas Krishnan, an individual based in Malaysia ("we", "us"). These terms sit alongside the Somni Terms of Service.

2. Who can take part

The Programme is by invitation only. You need a referral code issued to you by us, you must be at least 18, and you must live in Malaysia (or another country we agree to in writing). You must not promote your code to people you know or believe to be under 18. We may decline an invitation at our discretion, and may end your participation with 14 days' notice, or immediately if you break section 7.

3. What counts as a referral

A referral counts when a new Somni user enters your code when they sign up, or in the app within 14 days of creating their account. Only new accounts qualify; people who already have a Somni account cannot be referred. Each account can use one referral code only, and you cannot use your own code.

4. Rewards

You earn a one-time reward when a person you referred makes their first subscription payment at the regular (non-introductory) price. Free trials, introductory prices and discounted first periods do not count; if your friend starts with one, the reward is earned only when the first regular-price payment is made. The reward depends on the plan of that first regular-price payment:

PlanReward
Weekly planRM2
Monthly planRM6
Yearly planRM25
LifetimeRM25

Renewals and later payments do not earn further rewards. A lifetime purchase earns a one-time reward of RM25. Rewards are paid in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) unless we agree otherwise in writing.

5. Holding period and refunds

Each reward is held for 30 days after your friend's payment. If the payment is refunded or charged back during that time, no reward is earned. If a payment is refunded or charged back after we have paid you, or we later find that a reward was earned in breach of section 7, we may deduct that reward from your future payouts. If you have no future payouts, we may ask you to repay it within 30 days.

6. Payouts

Payouts are made once a month by bank transfer to the account you give us. A payout is made once your approved rewards reach at least RM20; smaller balances carry over to the following month. You are responsible for giving us correct payment details. As a guide, you will usually be paid one to two months after your friend's first payment.

7. Fair use

To keep the Programme fair, you must not:

  • create accounts yourself, or ask others to create accounts, just to earn rewards;
  • use misleading claims about Somni, or present yourself as Somni or as acting on our behalf;
  • send spam, or post your code on coupon or deal websites;
  • run paid ads that use the Somni name or bid on Somni search terms;
  • offer anyone money, gifts or other benefits for installing Somni, or for rating or reviewing it on the App Store or Google Play.

Rewards are paid by us, not by Apple or Google, and are not linked to app ratings or reviews. Taking part never requires you or your friends to allow tracking or advertising measurement. Apple and Google are not sponsors of the Programme.

We review referrals before paying. We may withhold or cancel rewards that we reasonably believe break these terms or involve fraud, duplicate accounts or abuse, and we may review unusually high numbers of referrals in a month before paying them.

8. Your relationship with us

Taking part does not make you our employee, agent or partner, and you cannot make promises or commitments on our behalf.

9. Taxes

You are responsible for any taxes that apply to the rewards you receive. We may ask for further details if the law requires them for tax reporting.

10. Privacy

We record which account used your code so that we can calculate rewards. We will tell you how many of your referrals signed up and paid, and what you have earned, but we do not share the identity or personal details of the people you referred. Your bank details are used only to pay you, and are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not share your referral, code-usage or bank details with advertisers.

11. Changes and ending the Programme

We may change the reward amounts or these terms, or end the Programme, with at least 14 days' notice to participants. Rewards for payments made before a change takes effect will be honoured under the terms that applied when the payment was made.

If the Programme ends, or your participation ends for any reason other than a breach of section 7, we will pay your full approved balance (including any amount below RM20), plus any rewards still in their holding period once they are approved, within 60 days after the end date. Approved rewards do not expire while the Programme runs. If we cannot pay because we have no valid bank details for you, we will remind you by email at least twice; balances unclaimed 12 months after the end date will be closed.

11A. Transfer

We may transfer these terms, and your approved balance, to a company that takes over the operation of Somni, as described in section 17 of the Somni Terms of Service. Your approved rewards and balance will be honoured in full.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Malaysia, and the courts of Malaysia have jurisdiction over any dispute. Before starting any claim, please email info@mysomni.app so we can try to resolve it within 30 days.
