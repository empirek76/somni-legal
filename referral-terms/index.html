Somni Referral Programme Terms

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Who runs the programme

The Somni Referral Programme ("Programme") is run by Srinivas Krishnan, an individual based in Malaysia, doing business as Empire K ("we", "us"). These terms sit alongside the Somni Terms of Service.

2. Who can take part

The Programme is by invitation only. You need a referral code issued to you by us, and you must be at least 18. We may decline or end anyone's participation at our discretion.

3. What counts as a referral

A referral counts when a new Somni user enters your code when they sign up, or in the app within 14 days of creating their account. Only new accounts qualify; people who already have a Somni account cannot be referred. Each account can use one referral code only, and you cannot use your own code.

4. Rewards

You earn a one-time reward when a person you referred makes their first paid subscription payment. Free trials do not count; if your friend starts with a free trial, the reward is earned when the trial converts to a paid subscription. The reward depends on the plan of that first payment:

PlanReward
Weekly planRM2
Monthly planRM6
Yearly planRM25

Renewals and later payments do not earn further rewards. Other purchases (for example lifetime access) are not covered by the table above; any reward for them is at our discretion. Rewards are paid in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) unless we agree otherwise in writing.

5. Holding period and refunds

Each reward is held for 30 days after your friend's payment. If the payment is refunded or charged back during that time, no reward is earned. If a payment is refunded after we have paid you, we may deduct that reward from your future payouts.

6. Payouts

Payouts are made once a month by bank transfer to the account you give us. A payout is made once your approved rewards reach at least RM20; smaller balances carry over to the following month. You are responsible for giving us correct payment details. As a guide, you will usually be paid one to two months after your friend's first payment.

7. Fair use

To keep the Programme fair, you must not:

  • create accounts yourself, or ask others to create accounts, just to earn rewards;
  • use misleading claims about Somni, or present yourself as Somni or as acting on our behalf;
  • send spam, or post your code on coupon or deal websites;
  • run paid ads that use the Somni name or bid on Somni search terms.

We review referrals before paying. We may withhold or cancel rewards that we reasonably believe break these terms or involve fraud, duplicate accounts or abuse, and we may review unusually high numbers of referrals in a month before paying them.

8. Your relationship with us

Taking part does not make you our employee, agent or partner, and you cannot make promises or commitments on our behalf.

9. Taxes

You are responsible for any taxes that apply to the rewards you receive.

10. Privacy

We record which account used your code so that we can calculate rewards. We will tell you how many of your referrals signed up and paid, and what you have earned, but we do not share the identity or personal details of the people you referred.

11. Changes and ending the Programme

We may change the reward amounts or these terms, or end the Programme, with at least 14 days' notice to participants. Rewards for payments made before a change takes effect will be honoured under the terms that applied when the payment was made.

These terms are governed by the laws of Malaysia.
