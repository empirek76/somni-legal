Welcome to Somni, a dream journaling and AI interpretation app developed and operated by Srinivas Krishnan, an individual based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Somni mobile application ("App"). By using Somni, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use Somni:

Account Information

  • Email address and password (hashed, never stored in plain text) when you create an account
  • Display name you choose during onboarding
  • Profile avatar image, if you choose to upload one

Birth & Astrological Information

  • Date of birth (collected at signup, required to verify age eligibility)
  • Birth time and birth city, if provided (used to generate your astrological, numerological, and Human Design charts)
  • Derived data calculated from the above: natal chart, birth coordinates (latitude/longitude), and birth timezone

Dream Journal Content

  • Dream entries you type or dictate, including the full text of your dreams
  • Voice recordings you make to capture a dream (processed immediately for transcription; recordings are not stored)
  • AI-generated interpretations, emotion tags, symbols, observations, and reflection questions associated with each entry
  • The date and time each dream is logged

Sensitive information

Dreams can reveal things about your health, mental or emotional state, religious beliefs, sex life or other sensitive matters. You decide what to write. From app version 1.6.17, we ask for your explicit consent with a tick-box before your first interpretation. In earlier versions, by recording a dream and requesting an interpretation, you explicitly consent to us processing that content, including sending it to our AI providers, for the sole purpose of providing Somni's features. You can withdraw this consent at any time by deleting the entry or your account.

Usage and Progress Data

  • Streak counts, experience points (XP), and realm level
  • Milestone achievements earned within the app
  • Dream calendar data and mood trend history
  • Number of shield items held

App Preferences

  • Notification preferences and scheduled reminder times
  • Appearance settings (light/dark theme)
  • In-app search history (stored locally on your device)

Subscription and Payment Information

  • Your Somni Pro subscription status
  • Payment transactions are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat; we never see or store your payment card details

Referral Programme (invited participants only)

  • Your referral code, your name and contact details, the bank account details you give us for payouts, your reward balance and payout history, and, for each person who used your code, their account ID, sign-up date and first-payment plan. People who use a code are told that their account is linked to it; referrers see only counts and amounts, never who signed up.

Device and Technical Information

  • Device type, operating system and app version, and crash and error reports (collected by Sentry)
  • App usage events, such as screens opened and features used, plus your approximate location (country and city) derived from your IP address (collected by PostHog for analytics)
  • Advertising identifier and a limited set of app events (install, app open, sign-up and purchase), collected by the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure our advertising. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only collected if you allow tracking when the app asks; on Android, it is your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings
  • Push notification token (used to deliver the notifications you have opted into)

4. Guest Mode

Somni allows you to use the app without creating an account ("Guest Mode"). In Guest Mode:

  • Your dream entries are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and are not synced to any server
  • Up to 2 AI interpretations are available; the text of those dreams is still sent to Anthropic for processing
  • If you later create an account, your guest dreams are automatically migrated to your Supabase account
  • If you uninstall the app without creating an account, your guest data is permanently deleted

5. Data Storage and Security

All account data is stored in Supabase, which uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit. Row-level security (RLS) policies ensure that each user can only access their own data — even at the database level, one user's dreams cannot be read by another user's session.

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, which uses bcrypt hashing.

Dream content sent to Anthropic or OpenAI for AI processing is transmitted over HTTPS. Under their API terms, these providers do not use it to train their models. Under their published API data policies, they may keep it for up to 30 days for safety and abuse monitoring, and then delete it. They may keep it longer where required by law or where content is flagged as violating their usage policies (Anthropic: up to 2 years for flagged content). We do not have a zero-data-retention arrangement with either provider.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account, all associated dream entries, profile data, and XP history are permanently deleted from Supabase within 30 days.
  • Guest data: Stored only on-device. Deleted when you uninstall the app or reset guest mode.
  • Voice recordings: Processed transiently for transcription and immediately discarded. Not stored on our servers.
  • Push tokens: Retained while your account is active and deleted upon account deletion or notification opt-out.
  • Referral payout records (your name, the amount, the date and the bank transfer reference) are kept for 7 years from the end of the year of payment, as Malaysian tax law requires. Your full bank details and other referral data are deleted when no longer needed to run the programme or pay you.

Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

For requests related to EU/EEA or UK data subject rights under GDPR or UK GDPR, or rights under US state privacy laws (including the right to opt out of "sharing" or targeted advertising, which you can exercise through the advertising opt-out above or by emailing us), please contact us at info@mysomni.app. We will respond within 21 days for PDPA requests and within one month for other requests, which may be extended where the law allows.

9. Push Notifications

Somni may send you push notifications to remind you to capture your dreams in the morning, and to alert you if your streak is at risk of being broken. You can change your notification preferences at any time in the app's Settings or in your device's system notification settings.

Separately, with the "Tips & offers" setting (Profile → Notifications), we may occasionally send news about new features and offers, at most once a week. This setting is off by default if you are in the UK, the EU/EEA or Switzerland, and you can switch it off at any time.
