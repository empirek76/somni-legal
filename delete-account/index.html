Delete Your Somni Account

Somni is developed and operated by Srinivas Krishnan (Srinivas Bala Krishnan, the developer listed on Google Play). This page explains how to delete your Somni account and data.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Delete in the app (fastest)

Open Somni, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.

Delete without the app

Email info@mysomni.app from the email address on your Somni account, with the subject "Delete my Somni account". We will confirm by email and delete your account. If you signed in with Apple using a private relay address, tell us that address.

Delete specific data (keep your account)

You can delete individual dreams at any time in the app: open the dream and tap Delete Dream. The dream and its interpretation, reading and art are permanently deleted. To delete your profile photo, birth details or other specific data, or the usage analytics and crash reports linked to your account, email info@mysomni.app from the email address on your Somni account and tell us what you want deleted. We will confirm by email and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile and birth details
  • All dream entries, interpretations, readings and generated art
  • Streaks, XP, milestones and preferences
  • Your push notification token

All of this is permanently deleted within 30 days. Usage analytics and crash reports linked to your account can be deleted on request by emailing us. Guest-mode dreams are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail.
